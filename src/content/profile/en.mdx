## What I do

I study Computer and Control Engineering at Sapienza University of Rome and am starting my second year. I develop web and mobile applications, integrate AI models, and work on authentication and access control.

This site documents what I build and learn. I am interested in mathematics, logic, and local AI. I run models on my computer and experiment with agents to understand how they handle context, memory, and tools. Outside programming, I ride horses.

## Experience and collaborations

### Anthon · since 2025

I am one of three co-founders and the product and development lead for [Anthon](/projects/anthon), an AI assistant for sports mental coaching, within Sincro Group.

I gathered client needs and translated them into requirements, interfaces, and application behavior. I selected the platform and tools and defined how the assistant uses the coach's material. I developed and published the first version, integrating AI models, document retrieval through RAG, and access management.

I now lead Anthon 2.0 and set its development priorities. I coordinate releases and beta testing, from collecting reports to checking fixes, and discuss requirements and conversation privacy with the team.

I work as a full-stack and AI developer on [Drivewise](/projects/drivewise), an MVP that helps people choose a vehicle. My focus is the backend, while my co-founder develops the frontend. I define requirements and design the data model, with code reviews shared across the team.
