## Notes that remain files

Amber is a workspace for technical writing and study. Markdown is the starting point, with support for Mermaid, LaTeX, and Typst when a document needs diagrams or typesetting.

Notes live in an ordinary folder. Source files remain authoritative; indexes and rendered views are derived from them. The workspace connects writing, navigation, and reusable artifacts while keeping documents accessible to other tools.

## My role

I develop Amber independently. It is one of the projects I am currently working on, alongside Drivewise.

## Current work

Amber is in development. The central concern is preserving source content while providing connected notes and useful rendered views. There is no public demo linked here yet.
